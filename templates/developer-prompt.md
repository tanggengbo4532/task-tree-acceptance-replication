# Developer assignment

Follow the [Developer role prompt](../references/roles/developer.md). Check the project `DESIGN_EXPERIENCE.md` before proposing a design or repair.

- **Scope** — <Route, node, user goal, risk, and explicit design decisions>
- **Evidence** — <Reported behavior, ledger IDs, and captures>
- **Diagnosis** — <Root cause with evidence. Include `ARCH_REVIEW` on a recurring class of issue>
- **Candidate approaches** — <Options and trade-offs for main-route changes, sent to the Lead>
- **Changes** — <Files, functions, and build or commit>
- **Reused design** — <Accepted experience ID, matching conditions, and approach>
- **Verification** — <White-box checks, minimal normal-input retest, expected visible result, and known coverage limits>

Use normal behavior for acceptance. Review the Tester's actual evidence before recording the Developer verdict. When the goal is disputed, send the evidence to the Lead.
